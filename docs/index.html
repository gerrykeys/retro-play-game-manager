Retro PlayApp guide
GUIDE Everything you can do, in short

Your retro games, tidy and ready to play

Retro Play Game Manager keeps your games in order: you add them, fix titles and covers, and install them with one click on ES-DE or MiSTer FPGA. Each page explains one task and shows it in an animation.

Start here

How it works

01

The archive

The folder with your games and everything that describes them: titles, covers, videos, saves. On your PC, a drive or a NAS.

02

The app

Shows the archive like a game store. Here you search, fix and complete: every change goes into the archive.

03

The frontend

Where you play: ES-DE on your PC or a MiSTer FPGA. The app installs games there and starts them.

Why it is made like this

  • The games stay normal files: you can use them without the app.
  • Each change rewrites only a small file, so even a huge archive on a NAS stays fast.
  • If another PC changed the same game, the app asks before overwriting.

If I edit a game in the app, does it change in ES-DE too?

Not right away: the change goes into the archive. ES-DE gets it when you install the game again.

Can I use the archive from several computers?

Yes, for example from a NAS. If two PCs change the same game, the app asks what to do. F5 reads the archive again.

Does uninstalling a game delete it?

No, it only removes it from the frontend: it stays in the archive. To delete it, use Delete from the archive.

Why does a game say «Cannot be installed»?

The frontend can’t load it: it doesn’t have that system, it takes none of the game’s formats (see Compatible formats), or it is an arcade game of another ROM Set. The tooltip says why.

Do I need an account for scraping?

For automatic scraping yes, a ScreenScraper account. To look things up on the web by hand, no.

Can I lose my saves?

No: the copies in the archive are never overwritten, and before bringing one back the app archives the frontend’s.

What languages is the app in?

English and Italian: choose in Settings → General.

Windows warns me the first time I start it, is that normal?

Yes: the app isn’t signed, so the first time SmartScreen asks for confirmation.
