GUIDE Everything you can do, in short

Your retro games, tidy and ready to play

Retro Play Game Manager keeps your games in order: you add them, fix titles and covers, and install them with one click on ES-DE, MiSTer FPGA, RetroArch, RetroPie or Batocera. Each page explains one task and shows it in an animation.

How it works

01

The archive

The folder with your games and everything that describes them: titles, covers, videos, saves. On your PC, a drive or a NAS.

02

The app

Shows the archive like a game store. Here you search, fix and complete: every change goes into the archive.

03

The frontend

Where you play: ES-DE or RetroArch on your PC, a MiSTer FPGA, a RetroPie or a Batocera. The app installs games there and starts them.
