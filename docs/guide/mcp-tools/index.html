Tools

MCP tools

Every tool the app gives an AI assistant through its MCP server: what it does, in plain words, and a question that makes the assistant use it.

How to use them

  • Turn the server on and connect your assistant: see MCP server for AI.
  • You don’t call the tools yourself: ask in your own words and the assistant chooses them. The questions are examples: a click copies one.
  • The same list is in the app, in Settings → MCP server → Show the tools.

Safety

  • Tools marked Read only only look. Those marked Makes changes change the archive, the frontend or a file, and you see every change in the window.
  • To delete games, or install, uninstall and import them, the assistant first shows you the plan and needs your confirmation.

Archive and search4

The open archive: its systems with how many games they have and their size, the ROM Sets and the frontend connected.

search_gamesRead only

Finds games with filters and sorting: system, genre, year, developer, favorites, what is missing…

game_statsRead only

Counts games and groups them by system, genre, developer, year, decade or rating; tells how many lack each detail.

get_gamesRead only

Everything about some games: details, media, files, collections, saves and how they stand in the frontend.

The games released on this day in past years, as On this day shows them.

year_in_reviewRead only

Your retro year: hours played, most played games, systems, genres and what kind of player you are.
