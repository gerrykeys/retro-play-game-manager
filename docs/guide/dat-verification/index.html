Verify with DATs

A DAT (No-Intro, Redump, MAME…) lists the good dumps of a system. The app compares your files with the DAT, without touching them.

  1. Open the archive’s menu (its name, top left).
  2. Choose Verify with DATs…
  3. For each system click Choose DAT…, then Verify all.
  4. For each system you see how complete it is and the state of its games.
  5. At the top choose how to count: Every dump or One version per game.
  6. Missing… lists the DAT’s games you don’t have.
  7. One click on a state shows those games in the library.

The states

  • Verified the files match the DAT.
  • Bad dump the DAT marks it as bad.
  • Differs from the DAT there are files too many or missing.
  • Not in the DAT, Unverifiable (7z, CHD), To verify.

Good to know

  • Verifying is fast even on a NAS.
  • A game’s state is also on its page and in the Filters.
