Frontends

Archive the frontend’s games

Does the frontend have games the archive doesn’t? You find them in «To archive» and import them with one click, with details and media.

  1. Click To archive in the sidebar.
  2. The button at the top imports them all (or choose which ones).
  3. Check and confirm.

Good to know

  • From ES-DE come details and media too, from the MiSTer only the files.
  • A single game is imported from its page, with Import into the archive.
