Settings and help

Release notes

Every version of the app since the first, newest first, with what it changed. In the app, Settings → About → Release notes opens this page.

0.0.9

Latest 24 November 2026 Download

Play your games on the cathode-ray TV, right in the room, and open an ’80s arcade for your arcade games.

  • Play on the Retro TV

    With ES-DE and RetroArch, Play starts the game on the television in the room, full screen or in the window, and Zoom says how close the camera comes. First the game goes into its console, which stays beside or under the TV.

  • Arcade Room

    Your arcade games on a 3D candy cabinet, in 5 illustrated ’80s arcades or in the dark. For vertical games the monitor turns, and Play starts the game right there. In the title bar, beside Retro TV, and in Desktop Mode.

And also

  • Retro TV has 12 televisions, famous ones too like the Sony Trinitron, Sony PVM and Commodore 1084, and 5 new illustrated rooms or an all-black one.
  • In Picture you adjust the tube’s effects, down to a sharp image.
  • In Games you browse the installed ones or search the whole archive, and the TV shows the one you stop on.
  • Retro TV is in the title bar, beside Desktop Mode, and shows every system except the arcade ones.

0.0.8

23 November 2026 Download

Turn on a cathode-ray TV full of your games, discover who made them, and watch the console take the game before it starts.

  • Retro TV

    The last view of the library, and a section of Desktop Mode, broadcasts your games’ videos on a cathode-ray television: channels, zapping, 6 TVs and 6 rooms. Press Play and you go inside the screen.

  • The people behind the games

    Follow composers, game designers and other authors through your games: each career becomes an animated score or blueprint. A click on the record searches YouTube for the soundtrack. The credits come from Wikidata, and nothing of your archive is sent.

  • The power-on ritual

    When you press Play, the console takes the game’s cartridge, disc or coin and turns on. Any key or click skips it, and you can turn it off in Settings → More settings.

And also

  • The Company map also shows people who make games on their own, in orange, where they were born.
  • The map finds the best-known companies first, then rare names one at a time.
  • The archive menu shows how much space the Web data takes.
  • The AI can explore the people and their careers too (MCP server).

0.0.7

22 November 2026 Download

See what the AI can do with your archive, and find the frontend’s settings all in one place.

  • What the AI can do

    In Settings → MCP server, Show the tools lists every tool with what it does and a question to ask the AI: a click copies it. You can search, and you see which tools make changes.

  • The frontend’s settings in one frame

    In Settings → Frontend, what applies to the connected frontend sits in one frame with its name: What gets installed (formats and ROM Sets), Emulators and commands, Where the games are. The other frontends stay outside.

And also

  • The frame reminds you that its settings stay saved even if you disconnect the frontend.
  • The guide has a site of its own, retroplay-gamemanager.com, with a page for each topic.

0.0.6

22 November 2026 Download

Add games by moving their files instead of copying them, and delete many games much faster.

  • Move instead of copy

    In Add games, Move the files instead of copying them empties the folder into the archive without taking twice the space. On the same disk it is almost instant. Off until you turn it on, and the app asks first.

And also

  • The files leave the folder only at the end, once the games are in the archive.
  • Deleting many games is much faster, with a bar showing how far it is.
  • Join all shows a bar and how many groups it has joined so far.

0.0.5

21 November 2026 Download

Choose the emulator and the command Retro Play starts your games with.

  • Emulators and commands

    For each system, the emulator Play starts its games with and its whole command line; a single game can have its own. ES-DE, RetroArch, RetroPie and Batocera. The frontend stays as it is.

And also

  • From a game’s menu, Emulator and command… opens them on that game.
  • Cheats turn on even when ES-DE’s command already gives RetroArch a configuration of its own.
  • RetroPie: the emulator chosen for a single game is now read right.

0.0.4

21 November 2026 Download

The portable version now updates itself too.

  • The portable updates itself

    Like the installed app: at start it downloads the new version and puts it in place of the old .exe, same folder, same name. Shortcuts and Start with Windows keep working.

And also

  • On a portable 0.0.3 or older? Download this version by hand once: from here on it updates itself.
  • If its folder can’t be changed, Settings → About says so and leads to the download page.

0.0.3

21 November 2026 Download

Themes for Desktop Mode, a world map of the companies, gaming journeys to share and the app starting with Windows.

  • Desktop Mode themes

    Colors, fonts, music, even what each screen is made of. Four themes included, an editor to make your own, and a guide to go further.

  • Company map

    The developers and publishers of your games on a world in the colors of the app. The missing places you set with a click.

  • Gaming journeys

    Games in a row, a note for each step. Shared as a .zip with no game files: whoever imports it plays the steps they have.

  • Start with Windows

    The app opens when you sign in and, closed, stays beside the clock.

And also

  • Journeys in Desktop Mode and on the phone too.
  • The app is in English by default: Italian is in Settings → General.
  • View buttons in two groups; the cover size sits at the bottom right of the grid.
  • Clearer More settings, each area in a panel of its own, and the release notes in Settings → About.
  • New MCP tools: themes, company map, journeys.

0.0.2

19 November 2026 Download

Your retro year, cheats, the phone as a second screen and a palette to do it all from the keyboard.

  • Your retro year

    Hours, favorite games and consoles, the times you play: your year told as a story, down to a card to share.

  • Play time

    The app times the games you start from it, on every frontend.

  • Cheats

    Find a game’s cheats in the libretro database, turn on the ones you want, and they start with the game. For RetroArch.

  • The phone as a second screen

    While you play: the clock, your notes, the manual, the cheats on and the RetroAchievements.

  • Command palette

    Ctrl K and a few letters: games, systems, views, settings and actions in one box.

  • More like this

    At the foot of each game’s page, the games of your archive most like it.

  • Discord status

    Your friends see what you’re playing. Off until you turn it on.

  • Check archive

    Finds the media no game uses any more and deletes them in one click.

And also

  • Desktop Mode: leave the joypad alone and the games’ videos play.
  • If a game doesn’t start, the app tells you why.
  • Notes for each game, on its page and on the phone.
  • The What’s new window after each update.
  • New MCP tools: cheats, notes, similar games, retro year, unused media.

0.0.1

19 November 2026 Download

The first public version: your retro games, tidy and ready to play.

  • The library

    Covers, list and timeline, fast even with tens of thousands of games.

  • Search and filter

    Instant search, saved filters, favorites and collections.

  • Details and media

    Edit one game or many at once. Covers, videos and manuals by dragging, pasting or from the web.

  • Scraping

    Details, covers and videos from ScreenScraper, with IGDB as an extra source.

  • Versions and formats

    One game in several regions or kinds of file, duplicates joined in a click. Arcade games by ROM Set.

  • Frontends

    Install, uninstall and play on ES-DE, MiSTer FPGA, RetroArch, RetroPie and Batocera.

  • Desktop Mode

    The app full screen, played with a joypad.

  • The phone as a remote

    Browse and start games from the sofa.

  • Saves

    Kept safe in the archive and brought back when you need them.

And also

  • Continue playing, On this day and the What should I play today? wheel.
  • Videos and screenshots on hover, and up to 6 games side by side.
  • Archive the games only the frontend has and choose the formats each platform takes.
  • Verify with DATs, with completeness and one version per game.
  • Export and import, the archive report, DIP switches, RetroAchievements and the MCP server.
  • The installed app updates itself. English and Italian, light and dark themes.
