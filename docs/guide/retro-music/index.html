Retro Music

Retro Music turns your games’ soundtracks into cassettes with their covers, to play in a 3D tape player. While one plays, the TV at the back shows the game’s video.

  1. At the foot of the sidebar, click Scenes, then See all, and choose Retro Music.
  2. Every game with a soundtrack is a cassette on the rack. Point at one to read its title.
  3. Click it: its case comes to you with the list of tracks. Drag it to turn it; Turn over shows side B.
  4. Click Play or a track: the case opens and the cassette goes into the player. The TV at the back shows the game’s video.
  5. Use the player’s keys or the bar at the bottom. Hold ◀◀ and ▶▶ to rewind and fast forward; stop twice ejects the cassette.
  6. Click the player to take it in hand and turn it any way you like: its keys still work.
  7. With Style choose the player (Hi-fi deck, Boombox, Personal player, Tape recorder, Car stereo) and the cassettes (Classic, Chrome, Clear, Neon, Pixel).
  8. Click + (Add a soundtrack) at the top, choose the game, then its audio files. You can also do it from the game’s page, in the Soundtrack card.

Good to know

  • The files (mp3, ogg, flac, wav, m4a…) are copied into the archive, in @music. On the game’s page, double click a track to rename it, or move it or take it away.
  • Keys: arrows and Enter to take and play, Space play and pause, P N track, , . back and ahead, E ejects, S the style. Joypad: A takes and plays, X play and pause, Y ejects, LB RB track, LT RT back and ahead.
  • It is also a section of Desktop Mode, for the joypad. The app never downloads soundtracks: it uses the files you have.
